10 P.M., Nov 24th, at a coffee shop in Dist 7, HCMC.

Me, a 1 year fresh graduate, currently being surrounded by the enthusiasm, the energy from the youngsters, the university students who are working together to finish their project, their works. I'm hearing the laughters, the enjoyment, the discussion, also, a faint sense of stress from them. And, I'm being honest to myself, I miss those time a lot: gathering with my friends to finish the last-semester project, or just discussing technical news, personal workflows with each other.

We rarely meet each other now, maybe twice or thrice per year. We still communicate, through messenger, sharing technical blogs, views, works, but not the kind of energy we have during those student year.

Life has been hustle and bustle lately. Everyday, a new technology is released. If I don't catch up, I will be fallen behind. This makes me crave for information daily: scrolling through hackernews, reading every technological posts, ask chatgpt to run a workflow of gathering news,... I will do everything, thinking to myself, these are good for me.

And, it's finally come. The day that I crash out. I cannot consume any of the knowledge. My forehead burns, and my brain can't consume what I read anymore. I try different ways, resting myself for 20-30 minutes to ease my head, stay away from the screen, taking a stroll, but, nothing seems to work. It feels like I hit a wall, a physical limitation of my brain, like a room that has been filled with water and about to overflow.

Why? Is this the price of being a developer? Is this because of AI? Just why?

Or, it's just me being childish. It's just complaints from someone that just step into life, out of school and cannot handle the stream.

Anyway, I realize that I can't hold everything on my mind, so I wrote system that can help me offload some of it. I read Building a Second Brain by Tiago Forte, Getting things done (first chapter of it, its writing style is not kind of my favor), adopt the LLM Wiki patterns, and finally, be able to build Janus, the agent that control my life.
